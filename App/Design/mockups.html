Grimoire in the HT Press house style

Three proofs of the same Inquiry page, drawn from the token file in the pull request. Pick one, or name what to change. The Mac app has all three behind Settings › Appearance › House style.

Mac in

A · Codex

Full press. Vellum page, ink type, Garamond everywhere, square corners. Red marks the selection, the kicker and the rule.

  • Dark mode: ink-black page, soot cards, linen type
  • Labels and lists also in EB Garamond
  • Titles in tracked Cormorant capitals

B · Marginalia

Light touch. Apple's white pages, rounded cards and San Francisco labels stay. Garamond carries titles and everything you write. Rubric red replaces the accent.

  • Closest to today's app
  • Breaks the “no sans” rule for labels on purpose
  • Titles in Cormorant, not capitals

C · Beltane Night

Beltane ultramarine night ground, linen type, red that glows. Always dark, whatever the Mac is set to.

  • The night ground from the house palette
  • Red on dark is #FF3232
  • Best for evening and ritual work

Not in these proofs yet: Hugh's woodcut marks from Figma “HT Brand” (they will sit beside the kicker and in empty pages) and Physis for astrological glyphs. Both need the files exported.

Every colour and size comes from App/Design/tokens.json. A test checks that all text meets WCAG AA contrast on every ground.
